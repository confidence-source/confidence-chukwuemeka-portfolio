A little about me

I pay attention to what people need to hear, and what an organisation needs to say.

I'm Confidence Chukwuemeka, a corporate communications and marketing professional with eight years of experience building brands, leading teams and shaping conversations across the nonprofit, development, technology and fintech sectors.

My work has taken me from growing digital audiences for fintech brands to translating research into policy-facing communications, producing national television campaigns and building relationships with media, government institutions, funders and civil society organisations.

I'm drawn to work that has a purpose, but I don't believe purpose is a substitute for good strategy. The message has to be sharp. The execution has to be thoughtful. And the results should mean something to the people the organisation exists to serve.

I'm observant, resourceful and comfortable bringing structure to complex work. I enjoy building teams and systems that make good communications repeatable, not dependent on last-minute heroics.
